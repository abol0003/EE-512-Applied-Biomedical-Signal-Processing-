def my_corr(x):
    rxx = np.correlate(x-np.mean(x), x-np.mean(x), mode='full')/len(x)
    return rxx

Q: How the different peaks are related to the control of the autonomic
nervous system?

The sharp peak of respiration (0.20–0.25 Hz, HF band) marks respiratory sinus arrhythmia and vagal (parasympathetic) modulation; the LF peak in cardiac intervals and blood pressure (~0.1 Hz) reflects baroreflex-driven oscillations (“Mayer waves”) with mixed sympathetic–parasympathetic influence; the very-low-frequency background in blood pressure (<0.04 Hz) reflects slower regulatory mechanisms.

Q: Do the positions and amplitude of the peaks confirm you previous
findings;

Yes. The high-frequency peak around the respiratory rate is strong in respiration and also visible in the interbeat intervals, confirming the coupling between breathing and heart rate. In mean blood pressure this peak is weaker, while the spectrum is dominated by low-frequency components, which agrees with the observation of slower and less regular oscillations in blood pressure and its inverse relation with interbeat intervals at rest.

from scipy.signal import windows
def my_psd(x, half_win=250):
    interval = np.arange(-half_win,half_win+1)+len(x)//2
    x_sub = x[interval]
    psd = np.abs(np.fft.fft(windows.hann(len(interval))*x_sub, 2048))
    return psd
